# Privacy policy

Prosefchi collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no
analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and nothing you do in the app
is sent anywhere. Nobody, including the people who make it, can see which
prayers you read or which reminders you set.

This covers the app, which appears on your device as Orthodox Prayer, and the
website at prosefchi.org.

## What is kept on your device

The app keeps:

- The language and text size you chose.
- Whether you have seen the welcome pages.
- Which reminders you switched on, and the times you set for them.
- The calendar of saints, feasts and readings it downloads, so the day's page
  and the prayers work with no signal.

All of it sits in the app's own private storage, is never uploaded, and goes
when you uninstall. On Android it can also be cleared without removing the app,
from Settings, then Apps, then this app, then Storage.

## What the app sends

The calendar is not built into the app. It is a file published on this site and
fetched when the app opens, and that request is the only thing the app sends
anywhere.

Asking a server for a file means it sees your device's internet address, the
time of the request, and which of the two languages you read. The app sends no
identifier of its own, because it has none, and no cookies. The site is served
by GitHub Pages, so that record is GitHub's. We are not given those logs and
cannot see them. GitHub's
[privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
describes what they do with them.

Nothing else the app does touches the network.

## Reminders

Reminders are worked out and scheduled on your device, using the notification
scheduling the OS provides. Nothing is pushed from a server, which is why a
reminder still arrives with no signal, and why the app never learns when you
pray or fast. Your time zone is read on the device, so that a reminder set for
seven in the morning arrives at seven in the morning.

## The website

No cookies and no analytics. The prayer pages are plain files. The day page
fetches the same calendar file the app does, logged by GitHub in the same way.

A prayer names the site its text came from, and the about page links to the
project on GitHub. Following either hands you to that site, which is governed by
its own privacy policy rather than by this one.

## Permissions

- Notifications, so a reminder you switched on can appear.
- Internet access, so the calendar can be downloaded.
- Starting at boot, so reminders survive a restart. Without it they would
  disappear the first time the device was turned off.
- Vibration, so a reminder can be felt as well as seen.

Nothing else. The app has no access to your contacts, your location, your
camera, your microphone, your files or your accounts.

## What others record

If Android's backup is turned on, it may include this app's settings and
reminders. That is the operating system copying the data above to your own
account, at your instruction, rather than us. Android's settings are where it is
turned off.

Installing from Google Play means Google records the install, as it does for
every app, and reports back to us in aggregate: how many installs and active
devices, in which countries, on which kinds of device, and, where you have
allowed Google to share usage and diagnostics, anonymised reports of crashes.
None of it identifies anyone and none of it comes from the app. It is Google's
collection, described in
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Installing the
APK from the project's GitHub releases instead means GitHub sees the download.

## Children

The app collects nothing from anybody of any age, so there is nothing held about
a child either.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version is published here and the date below
changes with it, and every past version is in the project's history. Questions
can be raised as an issue:
[github.com/Prosefchi/prosefchi/issues](https://github.com/Prosefchi/prosefchi/issues).

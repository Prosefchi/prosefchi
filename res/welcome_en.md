# Welcome

Glory to God. Thank you for downloading this Orthdox prayerbook app. This app is
free and open-source. It will always be. The Lord's word should be received by
everyone.

## Today

The "Today" page shows the saints and feasts, along with the Epistle and Gospel
of the day. It informs you if the day is a fast day. It also prompts you to pray
the prayer of the time.

## Prayers

The app has prayers taken from various online sources, both in Koine Greek and
modern English. It has the most common prayers of the day:

- Morning
- Midday
- Night
- Before/After Holy Communion

## Reminders

We are human, and sometimes we forget to fast or pray. You can set reminders so
that you can be reminded to fast or pray at the correct time for each occasion.

## Calendar

The calendar updates when the device connects online. Once the calendar is
downloaded, it stays on the device, so the saint of the day is available even
without an internet connection.

Either calendar can be chosen in settings. Both keep Pascha, and everything
counted from it, on the same day; only the fixed feasts move.

> The saints, feasts, and readings of the new calendar are sourced from the
> [calendar of the Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America](https://www.goarch.org/en/chapel/planner).
> The Archdiocese publishes the new calendar only, so those of the old calendar
> are sourced from [orthocal.info](https://orthocal.info/), which is available
> in English only.
